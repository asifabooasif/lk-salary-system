Loans & Advances

How Loan Auto-Deduction Works

✅ When you issue a loan, set the First Deduction Month

✅ When creating pay slip for that month (or later), loan installment automatically appears as deduction

✅ When you finalize pay slip as Paid, loan balance reduces automatically

✅ Example: Loan taken in August, first deduction month = August → When you create August pay slip in September, installment auto-deducts
